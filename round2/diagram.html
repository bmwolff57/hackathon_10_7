GET ME HOME NYC · HOW IT WORKS

One verified feed, only when the city needs it

A city-sponsored data API that turns official alerts, transit load and social signals into verified closures and smarter routes home, then delivers them inside apps people already use.

01

Activate

NYC Emergency Management declares event mode.

  • Parade, flood, blackout
  • Auto-expires
  • Off by default
02

Ingest

Pull every signal for the zone.

  • NYPD / DOT / MTA alerts
  • Station & phone density
  • Social text & photos
03

Verify

Score every closure by source. Social alone never closes a street.

  • Confirmed · Likely · Reported
04

Route & balance

Walk a little further to a quieter train or a pickup zone.

  • Spread people across options
  • "Leave now" vs "wait"
05

Deliver

Certified sponsors pull the public feed.

  • Maps, rideshare, socials
  • Kiosks, arena screens
  • SMS for any phone

Official source

Likely
